About me

Hi, I'm Leah

Founder of Renew & Revitalise Wellbeing.

For many years, I've worked alongside adults, young people and families, supporting them through some of life's most challenging times. Along the way, I saw how often people put their own needs last when life gets hard, and what a difference it makes when they take time to care for their body as well as their mind.

Lymphatic rolling has also been part of my own wellness journey. When I first tried these machines for myself, I loved how much lighter, calmer and more energised I felt afterwards, and how good it was to have time that was just for me. The more I experienced it, the more I wanted to understand how and why it works, so I completed CPD courses in Anatomy and Physiology of the Human Body and in Lymphatic Drainage. Now I'm excited to bring that knowledge, and the feel-good benefits of rolling, to you.

That's what inspired Renew & Revitalise. I wanted to create a calm, welcoming space where you can switch off, look after yourself and leave feeling more like you again.

Taking time for yourself isn't selfish. It's how you keep showing up for everything and everyone else.

I can't wait to welcome you to the studio.
