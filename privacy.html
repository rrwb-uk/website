Your information

Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information Renew & Revitalise Wellbeing collects, why we need it, and how we look after it. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Who we are

  • Renew & Revitalise Wellbeing, inside Extraordinhair, The Parade, Ridley Road, Bury St Edmunds. We are responsible for your personal information (the "data controller").
  • If you have any questions about your information, email us at hello@rrwb.uk.

What we collect

  • Your name and contact details (such as email address and phone number) when you book through Fresha.
  • Your booking history, packs and membership details, and records of payments and any cancellation fees.
  • Your confirmation that you've read and agree to our terms and conditions, including that none of the conditions listed in our FAQs apply to you.
  • Any health information you choose to tell us, for example during your consultation or about changes to your health, so we can keep your sessions safe.
  • Anything you send us by email.

Card details are saved and processed securely by Fresha. We never see your full card number.

Our website

  • Our website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn't have any forms.
  • It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Our booking links take you to Fresha, where Fresha's privacy policy and cookies apply.

How we use your information

  • To take and manage your bookings, packs and memberships, and to take payments. We need this to provide the service you've booked.
  • To keep you safe during your sessions. If you tell us about your health, we only use it for this, and we rely on your explicit consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time, but we may then not be able to offer you a session.
  • To keep financial records, as the law requires.
  • To send you news and offers, but only if you've agreed to this. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

  • Fresha, which provides our booking, payment and client record system.
  • Our accountant and HMRC, where needed for tax and accounting.
  • We never sell your information or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

  • Client records, including any health information, are kept while you're a client and for up to 3 years after your last visit.
  • Financial records are kept for 6 years, as required by HMRC.

Your rights

  • You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop or limit how we use it.
  • Email hello@rrwb.uk and we'll reply within one month.
  • If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

  • We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
