Aftercare

Looking after yourself afterwards

Thank you for rolling with us. Here's how to get the most from your session over the next day or so.

For the rest of today

  • Drink plenty of water to support your body's natural fluid balance.
  • Go for a gentle walk if you can. Movement keeps lymph flowing.
  • Take it easy. Some people feel very relaxed or a little tired.
  • Needing the loo more often afterwards is common.
  • Mild tenderness can happen after your first few sessions. It usually settles within a day.

Keep your lymph moving between sessions

Short walking breaks

Ankle circles

Calf raises

Deep belly breaths

Regular sessions tend to feel better than an occasional one. The Roll Shaper's maker recommends 2 to 3 sessions a week.

When to get help

If you notice unusual pain, new swelling, or redness and heat in one area, or you feel unwell, contact your GP or NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999. Please let us know too.
