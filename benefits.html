Benefits

Your body, rolled and revived

Our lymphatic rolling sessions use the Roll Shaper IR, a mechanical massage machine with a rotating drum of shaped wooden rollers. As you move through a series of guided positions, the rollers work over your feet, legs, buttocks, tummy, back and arms, using gentle, rhythmic pressure designed to encourage natural lymph flow. Built-in infrared warmth adds a soothing, cosy heat to every session.

What clients love about it

Feel lighter

Many clients say their legs feel less heavy and puffy after a session.

Smoother-looking skin

Regular rolling may help improve the look of dimpled skin and skin firmness.

Boost your circulation

The rolling motion stimulates blood flow, leaving skin feeling warm and refreshed.

Ease muscle tension

A great way to loosen up tight muscles after exercise or a long day.

Infrared warmth

Gentle heat that feels comforting and helps you relax into the session.

Relax and unwind

Rhythmic, full-body massage that many people find calming, and some say helps them sleep better.

Support your wellness goals

A feel-good addition to a healthy diet and active lifestyle.

Easy for everyone

The positions are simple and need no special fitness level, so it suits beginners, active people and older adults alike.

What the Roll Shaper IR is designed to do

  • Stimulate the lymphatic system and encourage natural lymph flow
  • Boost circulation and blood flow
  • Help legs feel less heavy, tired and puffy
  • Help improve the look of cellulite and skin firmness
  • Support your body-shaping goals, alongside a healthy diet and regular exercise
  • Promote relaxation, recovery and an overall sense of wellbeing

Lymphatic rolling is a wellness and relaxation service, not a medical treatment. Results vary from person to person. Please read our FAQs to check rolling is right for you.
