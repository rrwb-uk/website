What is the lymphatic system?

Your lymphatic system is a network of tiny vessels and nodes that runs throughout your body, alongside your blood circulation. It collects excess fluid from your tissues, filters it through your lymph nodes, and returns it to your bloodstream, playing an important part in keeping your body balanced and healthy.

Unlike your blood, which has your heart to pump it, lymph has no pump of its own. It relies on movement, muscle activity and breathing to keep it flowing. That's why long days sitting at a desk or standing on your feet can leave your legs feeling heavy, tired or puffy.

Lymphatic rolling uses gentle, rhythmic pressure to encourage that natural flow, helping you feel lighter, refreshed and more like you again.

How it works

Our lymphatic rolling sessions use the Roll Shaper Infrared, a mechanical massage machine with a rotating drum of shaped wooden rollers. As you move through a series of guided positions, the rollers work over your feet, legs, buttocks, tummy, back and arms, using gentle, rhythmic pressure designed to encourage natural lymph flow. Built-in infrared warmth can be switched on for a soothing, cosy heat, or left off if you prefer.
