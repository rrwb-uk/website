FAQs

Your questions, answered

What is lymphatic rolling?

It's a mechanical body massage on a machine with a rotating drum of shaped wooden rollers. You move through a series of guided positions so the rollers work over each area of your body, with gentle, rhythmic pressure and optional infrared warmth.

Who is it for?

Almost anyone over 18. The positions are simple and need no special skills, so it suits beginners, regular exercisers and older adults. There are some health conditions where it isn't suitable. See "Is there anyone who shouldn't book?" below.

Does it hurt?

It shouldn't. How it feels depends on your skin sensitivity and how tense your muscles are. Some people feel a little discomfort in their first sessions, which usually eases after a few visits. We can adjust the speed throughout, so just let us know.

How long is a session?

Our Express Roll lasts 18 minutes and our Signature Roll lasts 45 minutes, so you can choose a quick top-up or the full experience. Please allow about an hour for your first visit, as it includes your consultation and a guided session.

How often should I come?

For best results, we recommend 2–3 sessions a week. Our multi-session packs and monthly memberships are the best value way to do this.

Will it help me lose weight?

Rolling isn't a weight-loss treatment and doesn't burn fat on its own. Many clients enjoy it as part of a routine that includes a balanced diet and regular exercise.

What should I wear?

Close-fitting clothes such as leggings and a T-shirt, with no strings, toggles or loose buttons that could catch in the rollers. Please tie long hair back and remove all jewellery, including rings and bracelets, before your session.

Can I book with a friend?

Yes, we run dual sessions, so two people can roll at the same time.

How does membership work?

Choose a monthly membership and pay automatically each month through Fresha. Your sessions are ready in your account, plus a free Express Roll every month with every membership, and you can book them whenever suits you. Unused sessions don't roll over to the next month.

What if I'm unwell on the day?

Please don't come in if you have a fever, an infection, a fresh wound or a recent injury. Let us know as soon as you can and we'll do our best to rebook you once you're feeling better.

Is there anyone who shouldn't book?

Rolling isn't suitable if you:

  • are pregnant
  • have a heart condition, a circulatory disorder or uncontrolled blood pressure
  • have varicose veins
  • have had cancer treatment within the last 5 years
  • have had surgery in the last 6 months, or have a wound that hasn't fully healed
  • have a serious inflammatory condition
  • are in the first days of your period
  • have a hernia, broken capillaries (telangiectasia) or pinpoint skin bleeding (petechiae) in the area being treated
  • weigh over 120 kg (the machine's safe limit)

Please check with your GP or specialist before booking if you:

  • have lymphoedema or lipoedema
  • finished cancer treatment more than 5 years ago
  • take blood-thinning medication
  • have a kidney, liver or thyroid condition
  • have a pacemaker or another implanted device
  • are unsure whether rolling is right for you

When you book, you'll be asked to tick a box confirming that you've read our terms and conditions and that none of the conditions above apply to you.

Is it safe?

Yes, when used correctly. On your first visit, we'll guide you through every position step by step, and we're always close by in the studio if you need anything. The machine also has an emergency stop and a safety board that halts the rollers instantly if needed. The rollers are cleaned and disinfected after every client.
