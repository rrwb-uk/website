The small print

Terms & Conditions

By booking a session with Renew & Revitalise Wellbeing, you agree to the following terms. Please read them carefully before booking.

Eligibility

  • You must be 18 or over to use our services.
  • For safety, the maximum client weight is 120 kg. We may ask you to confirm your weight if needed.

Health and suitability

  • All clients must agree to our terms and conditions before their first session, and tell us about any changes to their health before later sessions.
  • Lymphatic rolling isn't suitable for anyone with the conditions listed in our FAQs (including pregnancy, heart or circulatory conditions, uncontrolled blood pressure, varicose veins, cancer treatment within 5 years, surgery within 6 months, serious inflammatory conditions, and localised hernia, broken capillaries or petechiae).
  • You are responsible for giving us accurate health information. If you have any of the conditions in our "Please check with your GP or specialist" FAQ, or you're unsure whether rolling is right for you, please speak to your GP or specialist before booking.
  • We reserve the right to refuse or stop a session if we believe it isn't safe for you.

Not a medical treatment

  • Lymphatic rolling is a wellness and relaxation service. It isn't a medical treatment and doesn't replace advice from a doctor.
  • Results vary from person to person and aren't guaranteed.

During your session

  • Please wear close-fitting clothing with no loose strings, toggles or buttons.
  • Long hair must be tied back and all jewellery removed.
  • Keep hands and fingers away from the moving rollers and follow our instructions at all times.
  • Tell us straight away if you feel any pain or discomfort. Sessions can be paused or stopped at any time.
  • We'll guide you through your first session, and a member of our team is always in the studio if you need help during later sessions.

Hygiene

  • All surfaces you touch are cleaned and disinfected between every client.

Bookings and payment

  • All bookings and payments are made online through Fresha, using the booking link on our website.

Your first visit

  • Every new client's first visit is an Express Roll, or a Dual Express Roll if you'd like to come with a friend or family member, booked as a one-hour appointment. It includes your consultation and a guided session, where we take you through each position step by step.
  • From 17 November to 31 December 2026 it's £10 (Dual £18, £9 each), and from 1 January 2027 it's £15 (Dual £27, £13.50 each).
  • The introductory price is for new clients only, one per person, and can't be combined with any other offer.

Cancellations and rescheduling

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or change your appointment, and we'll happily reschedule it for you.
  • Late cancellations (less than 24 hours' notice) and no-shows may be charged 50% of the price you booked at. A card is saved when you book, and nothing is charged unless a fee applies.
  • First-visit Express Rolls and Dual Express Rolls are the exception: late cancellations and no-shows for these are charged 100% of the price you booked at.
  • If you're unwell on the day (for example with a fever or an infection), please don't come in. Let us know as soon as you can and we'll do our best to rebook you. Fees for illness are at our discretion.
  • Multi-session packs must be used within these times from the date of purchase: 4 sessions within 6 weeks, 8 sessions within 10 weeks, and 12 sessions within 16 weeks. Unused sessions can't be refunded or carried over once a pack expires.

Monthly memberships

  • Memberships are paid monthly by automatic payment through Fresha.
  • Each membership includes its monthly sessions plus one free Express Roll per person each month.
  • Sessions must be used within the billing month. Unused sessions don't roll over to the next month.
  • To cancel your membership, please give us one month's notice before your next payment date.
  • Membership sessions follow the same cancellation and rescheduling terms as all other bookings.
